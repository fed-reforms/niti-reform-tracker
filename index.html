From permission to trust

The reforms on this tracker follow one line of thinking. A committee reviews the rules, lays down principles, and those principles flip the default from permission to trust.

Jan Vishwas Siddhant · laid down by the High Level Committee

Five principles of trust-based regulation

Laid down by the High Level Committee for every existing and future Act, rule and regulation, to be implemented in a time-bound manner.

  1. verified_user1
    Licensing and inspectionsRisk-based: self-registration, perpetual validity, third-party checks
  2. gavel2
    Proportionate penaltiesMinor offences decriminalised; jail only for serious harm
  3. calendar_month3
    Regulatory cycleA fixed annual calendar, consultation and lead time to comply
  4. analytics4
    Regulatory impact assessmentEvery rule tested for compliance cost and enforcement burden
  5. hub5
    Digital complianceInteroperable systems; all rules on IndiaCode and the e-Gazette
